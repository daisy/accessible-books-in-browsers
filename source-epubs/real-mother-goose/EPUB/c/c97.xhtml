Shoeing

Shoe the colt,

Shoe the colt,

Shoe the wild mare;

Here a nail,

There a nail,

Yet she goes bare.

Betty Blue

Little Betty Blue

Lost her holiday shoe;

What shall little Betty do?

Give her another

To match the other

And then she’ll walk upon two.

That’s All

There was an old woman sat spinning,

And that’s the first beginning;

She had a calf,

And that’s half;

She took it by the tail,

And threw it over the wall,

And that’s all!
