# About the demos

The demos show the reader and the converter working on real books. Each book was converted from
the EPUB in [`source-epubs/`](../source-epubs/) without changing its words: the converter changes
markup and format only. The books belong to their authors and rights holders, listed below. If you
hold the rights to one of them and want it changed or removed, [open an issue](https://github.com/daisy/accessible-books-in-browsers/issues).
