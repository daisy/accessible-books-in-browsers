# About the demos

These are real books converted with this project. The converter doesn't change any of the book's
text, just its format. The original EPUBs are in [`source-epubs/`](../source-epubs/).

The books belong to their authors and rights holders, listed below.

## The books

Contribution.

**Moby-Dick**, Herman Melville. EPUB by Dave Cramer.

Minister's Office, and implemented by the Department of Social Services. In the book: প্রণয়নে -
